Strategy Lab — compare draft paths

Define a positional plan for your first several picks (e.g. RB,RB,WR,WR,QB vs RB,WR,WR,RB,TE). The lab runs many mock drafts (rivals pick by ADP + noise, keepers removed) and reports, for each of your picks, the tier of player you'd realistically land and how often your ideal targets survive. Use it to answer questions like "if 4 WRs I like keep surviving to round 3, should I go RB-RB early?"

Leagues

Create, edit, or delete league profiles. Saved to the cloud so any device sees the same list. AEO-Keepers can't be deleted here.

Connect a Sleeper league

Pulls owners and current rosters from a public Sleeper league ID. Sleeper doesn't expose ADP/projections or a reliable draft-type flag, so this fills the form below for you to review — nothing saves until you click Save.

Connect an MFL league

Pulls owners and current rosters from a public MyFantasyLeague league ID — no login needed. MFL doesn't expose ADP/projections, a draft-type flag, or a keeper flag, so this fills the form below for you to review — nothing saves until you click Save.

Import from Yahoo

Paste a league id — the number in football.fantasysports.yahoo.com/f1/123456 — or a full league key like nfl.l.123456 for a past season. A public league needs no Yahoo sign-in: the Worker signs the request with the app's own credentials. Connect an account below only if the league is private.

League members

      
Accounts you create here can run their own board, keep their own queue, rankings and opponent read, and save their own mocks. They can't change keepers, trades, league settings or commissioner data, and they can't see anyone else's rankings or draft.

      

      

        
        
        
        
      

    

    

      
What's shared, what's yours

      

        
Shared with everyone in the league — league settings and rosters, who's keeping whom, and pick/player trades. These are facts about the league, so everybody sees the same board setup.

        
Private to your account — your custom rankings, your queue, your per-owner tendency read, and your in-progress draft. Nobody else can load them, including the commissioner.

        
Before any account exists the app works exactly as it always has, with one shared save file. The read-only guest link (?guest=1) keeps working either way.
