Schedule and usable games

Decision context

This is the layer FantasyPros does not know: what this team is trying to do, how this league awards draft position, and which waiver route applies. It is saved with every ticket so our Waiver Lab sessions become reusable history.

League data sources

One normalized league snapshot powers FAAB now and is the foundation for future lineup, waiver, trade, and roster tools. Yahoo is the authoritative source for ownership and newly dropped players. FantasyPros MyPlaybook supplies the roster plus its weekly projections, injuries, opponents, ECR, and schedule context; its matchup feed does not enrich every Yahoo waiver player. When an available player lacks projection/ECR, Draft Lab shows a low-confidence Yahoo-ownership fallback instead of silently treating him as a zero-point player. If neither source returns a current waiver pool, Draft Lab keeps the last good pool instead of replacing it with an empty result.

Strategy Lab — compare draft paths

Define a positional plan for your first several picks (e.g. RB,RB,WR,WR,QB vs RB,WR,WR,RB,TE). The lab runs many mock drafts (rivals pick by ADP + noise, keepers removed) and reports, for each of your picks, the tier of player you'd realistically land and how often your ideal targets survive. Use it to answer questions like "if 4 WRs I like keep surviving to round 3, should I go RB-RB early?"

Leagues

Create, edit, or delete league profiles. Saved to the cloud so any device sees the same list. AEO-Keepers can't be deleted here.

Connect a Sleeper league

Pulls owners and current rosters from a public Sleeper league ID. Sleeper doesn't expose ADP/projections or a reliable draft-type flag, so this fills the form below for you to review — nothing saves until you click Save.

Connect an MFL league

Pulls owners and current rosters from a public MyFantasyLeague league ID — no login needed. MFL doesn't expose ADP/projections, a draft-type flag, or a keeper flag, so this fills the form below for you to review — nothing saves until you click Save.

Connect a Fantrax league

Pulls owners and current rosters from a Fantrax league ID — no login or app approval needed, unlike Yahoo. Fantrax does report its draft type and roster size, so those are filled in too. It carries no drafted round or keeper flag, so players import as FA/NONE — set keepers on Teams & Keepers afterwards. Nothing saves until you click Save.

Connect Yahoo (in progress)

This is where Yahoo gets connected — admin only, one grant covers every Yahoo league. Click Connect Yahoo account, approve in the tab that opens, then come back and click List my leagues. The FAAB Lab uses this connection for its best-effort Tuesday roster and waiver-pool sync; full league-profile import is still separate work.

If listing fails with additional_authorization_required, Yahoo is saying the grant this token was issued under has no fantasy access. A refresh token can never gain a permission it wasn't granted, so refreshing or reconnecting on top of the old grant won't help — and Yahoo skips the consent screen for an app you've already approved, quietly reissuing on the same grant. Two things to check, in order: (1) Check status below shows which client id this Worker actually holds — confirm it matches the app you edited in the Yahoo Developer console; (2) if it matches, revoke the app at Yahoo account security → apps, click Disconnect here, then Connect again so Yahoo has to ask you fresh.
